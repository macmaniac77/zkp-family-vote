Step 1 · Check your invite

Paste the full link the organizer sent, or just the long code after invite=.

Step 2 · Create your secret key

We generate this on your device. The public half is what gets registered — you don’t need to copy the public key by hand.

🔑
Secret vs public

Secret = keep forever, never upload. Public = safe for the server. Like a password vs a username.

Public key (will be registered)
Fingerprint

Secret is hidden until the next step so you focus on saving it safely.

Step 3 · Save your secret

Stop and save. If you lose this secret, you cannot vote. The server cannot recover it.

Step 4 · Register on the server

One click sends your public key with the invite. This does not cast a vote. It only puts you on the eligible list.

Step 5 · Vote

You’re registered. Voting opens when the organizer freezes the roster and clicks Open. Leave this tab open and hit refresh, or come back later with the same device (your key is saved here if you used the backup).
