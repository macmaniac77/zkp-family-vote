Step-by-step (everyone)

1

Organizer sets up

Pick a long admin passphrase (save it!). Create an election with a title and choices.

Organizer only
2

Organizer sends invites

One invite link per person (e.g. “Alex”). Copy each link and send it. Links are shown once.

Organizer only
3

Voter creates a key

Open your invite link. The wizard generates a secret key on your device — not on the server.

Each voter
4

Voter saves the secret

Download or copy the backup. If you lose it, you can’t vote. The wizard won’t continue until you confirm.

Each voter
5

Voter registers the public half

Only the public key is uploaded. Your name is not tied to a ballot on the chain. Invite is used up.

Each voter
6

Organizer opens voting

When everyone has registered, freeze the roster and open ballots. No new people after this.

Organizer only
7

Voters cast ballots

Pick a choice. Your device signs a proof. Server never gets your secret or writes your name next to the vote.

Each voter
8

See results on the chain

Tally is public. Ballots are anonymous. Everything lands in a hash-linked file on the server.

What you must never mix up

Secret key

Stays with you. Used to sign. Never chat/email it.

Public key

Safe to send to the server. Wizard sends it for you at register.

Invite link

One-time ticket from the organizer. Not your vote.

Admin passphrase

Organizer only. Not the same as a voter secret key.
